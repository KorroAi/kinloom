# Code of conduct

Kinloom is made by people who build games, for people who build games. Be the collaborator you would want on your
own project.

- Be kind and specific. Criticize code and ideas, never people.
- Assume good faith, and ask before you assume.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination or sexual content, in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere the
  project is represented.
- Respect people's time: search before you open an issue, and give the steps that reproduce a bug.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block people who
keep breaking them.

To report a problem privately, write to contact.korro@gmail.com. Reports are read by the maintainers only.
