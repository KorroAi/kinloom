## Kinloom
AI features in this game go through Kinloom. Read the Kinloom section of `AGENTS.md` and `kinloom/docs/reference.md` before changing them; the `kinloom-integrate` skill has the step-by-step workflow. Useful commands: `kinloom validate`, `kinloom test`, `kinloom doctor`, `kinloom inspect`; MCP server: `kinloom mcp`.
