# Kinloom developer interviews (15 minutes)

Rules: ask about their past, not about our idea. No pitch before question 7. Never ask "would you use/buy X?" — people are polite. Write down their exact words.

1. Tell me about the game you're working on. What NPCs or enemies does it have?
2. Walk me through the last NPC behavior you built. How did you do it?
3. Roughly how long did that take? What was the hardest part?
4. What have you tried or bought to help (assets, tutorials, frameworks)? What happened?
5. Did you ever cut or simplify a feature because NPC behavior was too much work? Which one?
6. What do players or testers say about your NPCs?
7. If that problem disappeared, what would it change for your game?
8. (Only now, 2 sentences:) I'm building a tool where NPCs notice, react, remember and spread rumors without scripting each behavior. Would you like to see the demo when it's ready?

After the call, fill one row in interviews.csv:
- pain_unprompted = yes only if they described NPC behavior as painful BEFORE question 8.
- would_pay_signal = one of: none / asked_for_demo / offered_to_test / asked_about_price / offered_to_pay.
